Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. No Physical Goods

VYRO sells no physical products. Nothing is shipped by post or courier, and no shipping charges apply to anything we offer. Everything we provide is digital.

2. How Delivery Works

  • Our apps are downloaded and installed instantly from our official channels, such as our Telegram channel at t.me/VYRO101;
  • Paid digital plans and services, where offered, are delivered digitally — inside the app, or via Telegram — within 24 hours of confirmed payment. In most cases delivery is much faster.

3. Delivery Delays

If a paid service has not been delivered within 48 hours of your confirmed payment, you are entitled to a full refund under our Cancellation & Refund Policy. Please also feel free to contact us — in many cases a delay simply means we need a detail from you to activate your plan.

4. Making Sure You Receive Your Service

To avoid delays, please make sure the details you share at the time of purchase (such as your Telegram username) are correct. Digital delivery does not depend on your physical location.

5. Contact

For any delivery question, please reach us through our Contact page or directly on Telegram.
